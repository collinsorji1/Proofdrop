Questions

What is ProofDrop?

A verification layer for last-mile delivery. It confirms the parcel reached the right place, the right person, and that any cash collected was correct.

How does the handoff code work?

Each delivery gets a 4-digit code that goes to the recipient. The rider can only complete the drop by entering it at the door.

Do riders need to be approved?

Yes. Rider accounts are reviewed before they can take deliveries. Merchant accounts are active straight away.

What if a rider says they delivered but the customer disagrees?

Every delivery keeps its location, time, photo and cash record, so there's evidence to settle it.
